## Follow-up: interact with the physical shop

User requested actual interaction with the shop. Replace the generic hand reach with object choreography derived from the painted shelf, bin, hook and counter positions. A tray lifts from the preparation shelf and returns; a cloth moves from its hook across the shelf and returns; a tin travels from the stock bin to the display counter and returns to the bin. The existing real customer queue and single-parcel handoff remain authoritative and interrupt these cosmetic chores; this change does not add purchases or modify the runtime.

`shopAction` separates the object's physical position from the hand: before pickup it remains at its source, during use it follows the palm, and after release it remains at its destination while the hands withdraw. Home and held objects share one tool painter and scale, so the transition does not resize the object. Coordinates are derived from scene geometry and converted to avatar-local contacts. Source and destination are the same holder for reusable tools, avoiding a jump at cycle wrap.
